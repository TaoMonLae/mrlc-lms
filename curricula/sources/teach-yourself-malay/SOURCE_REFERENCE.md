# Source Reference and Adaptation Notes

## Source supplied by the user

**Teach Yourself Malay**, EPUB edition containing scanned page images. The user represented that they hold all rights necessary to convert the material into a course. This package does not independently verify ownership and does not redistribute the original EPUB or its page scans.

## Source structure retained

The course preserves the source sequence:

1. Pronunciation and survival Malay
2. Welcome and introductions
3. Arrivals
4. Personal information
5. Work and school
6. Family
7. Sightseeing
8. Invitations
9. Directions
10. Changing money
11. Transport
12. Hotels
13. Clothes and bargaining
14. Restaurants
15. Supermarket and recipes
16. Hobbies and leisure
17. Healthcare, weather, and telephone communication

## Transformation

The course is an instructional adaptation rather than a page-for-page reproduction. Long dialogues and explanations were condensed into short interactive speaking prompts. The source progression, communicative functions, core vocabulary, and major sentence patterns were retained.

## Modernization policy

- Standard modern Malaysian spelling is used, including `boleh`, `selesa`, `kereta`, `hotel`, and `e-mel`.
- Diacritics used in the source to indicate pronunciation are removed from normal spelling.
- Prices and services use ringgit and contemporary payment language.
- Obsolete web, fax, and travel references are replaced where needed by current terms such as Wi-Fi, card payment, and e-mail.
- Scenarios are civilian, inclusive, and appropriate for adult and secondary learners.
- Malay phrases are written for Malaysian usage while avoiding highly local slang in early units.
