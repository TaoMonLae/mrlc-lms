# Teach Yourself Malay: Practical Speaking Course

## Course profile

- **Target range:** A1-B2
- **Primary skill:** practical speaking
- **Secondary skills:** listening recognition, sentence building, functional vocabulary, and reading short prompts
- **Recommended duration:** 17-34 weeks

## Course design

Every unit contains four lessons:

1. **Vocabulary Builder** - six core words or phrases.
2. **Core Conversation** - six situation-specific lines.
3. **Sentence Practice** - six complete sentence patterns.
4. **Daily Speaking Scenario** - six realistic responses.

Each lesson can be completed digitally in 8-15 minutes and then extended with teacher-led speaking practice.

## Suggested weekly routine

- Day 1: Vocabulary Builder and pronunciation shadowing.
- Day 2: Core Conversation with pair repetition.
- Day 3: Sentence Practice with substitution drills.
- Day 4: Daily Speaking Scenario and role-play.
- Day 5: Review, recorded speaking task, or live assessment.

## Speaking extension

After each digital challenge, the learner should say the selected answer aloud twice: first slowly, then at natural speed. For pair work, one learner reads the English situation and the other gives the Malay response without viewing the options.

## Assessment checkpoints

Use a short performance task after Units 4, 8, 12, and 16. Score each category from 0-4:

- Task completion
- Intelligibility
- Vocabulary accuracy
- Sentence control
- Interaction and repair strategies

A score of 14/20 indicates readiness to continue. Learners below the threshold should repeat the relevant scenario lessons and mastery review.

## Final speaking tasks

Learners should be able to:

- introduce themselves and describe family, education, and work;
- arrange a visit and respond to an invitation;
- ask for directions and use public transport;
- handle hotels, shopping, restaurants, and supermarkets;
- discuss hobbies and routines;
- explain common symptoms and arrange basic healthcare.
