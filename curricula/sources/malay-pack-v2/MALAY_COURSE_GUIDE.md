# Malay Speaking: Beginner to Advanced

**Platform:** MRLC Learning Quest
**Course code:** `MRLC-MALAY-SPEAKING-A1-C1-V1`  
**Scope:** 12 units · 48 lessons · 384 challenges  
**Variety:** Malaysian Malay / Bahasa Melayu  


## Historical source reference

The practical beginner themes are cross-referenced to the 1943 War Department publication *Malay: A Guide to the Spoken Language* (TM 30-339):

- https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-W-96478719d96146672c586f0d55523121/pdf/GOVPUB-W-96478719d96146672c586f0d55523121.pdf

The source uses old spelling and wartime/colonial framing. This pack does not reproduce it as current Malay. All learner-facing content uses modern Malaysian Malay, and a separate companion course, `MRLC-MALAY-GOVINFO-GUIDE-V1`, teaches the source sequence through current civilian scenarios. See `SOURCE_REFERENCE.md` and `source-to-course-mapping.tsv`.

## Course design

Each unit follows the same speaking cycle:

1. **Vocabulary Sprint** — 10 high-frequency words or expressions.
2. **Daily Speaking Sentences** — 8 useful sentences for real situations.
3. **Scenario Dialogue** — 6 short conversational exchanges.
4. **Sentence Workshop** — 8 grammar, word-order, register, or translation drills.

Every answer option includes matching `audioText`. Learners should listen, choose, repeat twice, and then use the sentence in a short role-play.

## Pronunciation quick guide

- Malay spelling is relatively regular. Keep vowels clear and avoid heavily stressing one syllable.
- **c** sounds like *ch* in “chair”; **g** is always hard as in “go.”
- **ng** is like the end of “sing”; **ny** is similar to *ny* in “canyon”; **sy** is like *sh* in “ship.”
- The letter **e** may sound like the vowel in “bed” or a relaxed schwa; learners should imitate the provided/local audio model.
- Use standard forms first. Conversational Malaysian Malay often shortens **tidak → tak**, **sudah → dah**, **mahu → nak**, and **sahaja → saja**. Teach these as listening variants, not as replacements in formal writing.

## Recommended pacing

- **Intensive:** 12 weeks, one unit per week, 4–5 sessions weekly.
- **Standard:** 24 weeks, two weeks per unit, 3 sessions weekly.
- **Session:** 25–40 minutes of Learning Quest plus 10–15 minutes of partner speaking.
- Move forward when a learner scores at least 80% and can perform the role-play with limited prompting.

## Unit map

### A1 · Unit 1: Greetings and Introductions

Meet people, exchange basic personal information, and handle polite first conversations.

**Grammar focus:** Personal pronouns; ialah/adalah avoided in simple identity sentences; question words siapa and mana; bukan for noun negation.

**Can-do outcomes:** Greet people at different times of day; Introduce yourself and another person; Ask and answer basic personal questions.

**Speaking performance:** Meet a new classmate and hold a 60-second introduction without reading.

**Vocabulary:** `hai` — hello, `selamat pagi` — good morning, `selamat tengah hari` — good afternoon, `selamat petang` — good evening, `selamat malam` — good night, `terima kasih` — thank you, `tolong` — please / help, `maaf` — sorry, `tumpang tanya` — excuse me; may I ask, `jumpa lagi` — see you again.

**Core sentences:**

- Nama awak siapa? — Ask a new friend for their name.
- Nama saya Amina. — Introduce yourself as Amina.
- Apa khabar? — Ask how someone is.
- Khabar baik, terima kasih. — Say that you are well and thank the person.
- Awak dari mana? — Ask where someone is from.
- Saya dari Myanmar. — Say that you are from Myanmar.
- Gembira berkenalan dengan awak. — Say that you are pleased to meet someone.
- Boleh ulang sekali lagi? — Politely ask someone to repeat.

### A1 · Unit 2: Family, Time and Daily Routine

Talk about family members, your home, the clock, weekdays, and repeated daily activities.

**Grammar focus:** Possession with noun + saya; time with pada pukul; frequency with setiap and biasanya; sequence with sebelum and selepas.

**Can-do outcomes:** Name close family members; Tell the time and day; Describe a simple daily routine.

**Speaking performance:** Describe your weekday routine from waking up to going to bed.

**Vocabulary:** `keluarga` — family, `ibu` — mother, `bapa` — father, `adik` — younger sibling, `rumah` — home / house, `bangun` — wake up, `sarapan` — breakfast, `bekerja` — work, `pukul` — o'clock / time marker, `tidur` — sleep.

**Core sentences:**

- Saya bangun pada pukul tujuh. — Say that you wake up at seven.
- Saya tinggal bersama ibu bapa saya. — Say that you live with your parents.
- Saya bersarapan sebelum pergi kerja. — Say that you eat breakfast before work.
- Saya pergi ke kelas setiap hari. — Say that you go to class every day.
- Pukul berapa sekarang? — Ask what time it is.
- Sekarang pukul dua setengah. — Say that it is half past two.
- Hari ini hari apa? — Ask what day it is today.
- Saya biasanya tidur awal. — Say that you usually sleep early.

### A2 · Unit 3: Food and Eating Out

Order meals, explain preferences and dietary needs, and pay at a restaurant or food stall.

**Grammar focus:** Requests with mahu and boleh; negation with tidak; quantity phrases; imperatives softened with tolong and jangan.

**Can-do outcomes:** Name common foods and tastes; Order politely; Explain a dietary restriction.

**Speaking performance:** Order a full meal, request one change, and ask for the bill.

**Vocabulary:** `nasi` — rice, `air` — water / drink, `ayam` — chicken, `ikan` — fish, `sayur` — vegetables, `pedas` — spicy, `manis` — sweet, `lapar` — hungry, `dahaga` — thirsty, `menu` — menu.

**Core sentences:**

- Boleh saya lihat menu? — Ask to see the menu.
- Saya mahu nasi ayam dan air kosong. — Order chicken rice and water.
- Tolong jangan terlalu pedas. — Ask for the food not to be too spicy.
- Saya tidak makan daging. — Say that you do not eat meat.
- Ada makanan vegetarian? — Ask whether vegetarian food is available.
- Boleh bungkus? — Ask for takeaway.
- Boleh saya minta bil? — Ask for the bill.
- Berapa jumlah semuanya? — Ask how much the total is.

### A2 · Unit 4: Shopping, Money and Directions

Compare products, pay for purchases, and ask for or give straightforward directions.

**Grammar focus:** Demonstratives ini/itu; comparatives with lebih; location with di; movement with ke; direction verbs belok and terus.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask prices and sizes; Handle cash or card payment; Follow and give basic directions.

**Speaking performance:** Buy clothing at a market, negotiate politely, then ask how to reach the bus station.

**Vocabulary:** `harga` — price, `murah` — cheap, `mahal` — expensive, `tunai` — cash, `kad` — card, `resit` — receipt, `kedai` — shop, `kiri` — left, `kanan` — right, `terus` — straight ahead.

**Core sentences:**

- Berapa harganya? — Ask the price of an item.
- Boleh kurang sedikit? — Politely ask whether the price can be reduced.
- Saya tengok-tengok sahaja. — Say that you are only looking.
- Ada saiz yang lebih besar? — Ask for a larger size.
- Saya mahu bayar dengan kad. — Say that you will pay by card.
- Di mana stesen kereta api? — Ask where the train station is.
- Belok kiri di lampu isyarat. — Tell someone to turn left at the traffic light.
- Jalan terus, kemudian belok kanan. — Tell someone to go straight and then turn right.

### B1 · Unit 5: Transport, Travel and Accommodation

Plan journeys, buy tickets, manage delays, check in, and solve common hotel problems.

**Grammar focus:** Future intention with akan; completed actions with sudah; belum for not yet; polite requests with boleh and tolong.

**Can-do outcomes:** Buy and confirm tickets; Ask about routes and delays; Check into accommodation and report a problem.

**Speaking performance:** Travel from an airport to a hotel and resolve a room issue at reception.

**Vocabulary:** `bas` — bus, `kereta api` — train, `teksi` — taxi, `tiket` — ticket, `stesen` — station, `lapangan terbang` — airport, `hotel` — hotel, `bilik` — room, `tempahan` — reservation, `lewat` — late.

**Core sentences:**

- Saya mahu satu tiket ke Kuala Lumpur. — Buy one ticket to Kuala Lumpur.
- Kereta api bertolak pukul berapa? — Ask what time the train departs.
- Di platform mana? — Ask which platform to use.
- Tolong hantar saya ke hotel ini. — Ask a taxi driver to take you to the hotel.
- Saya ada tempahan atas nama saya. — Say that you have a reservation under your name.
- Boleh saya daftar masuk sekarang? — Ask whether you can check in now.
- Penghawa dingin di bilik saya tidak berfungsi. — Report that the air conditioner is not working.
- Bas lewat tiga puluh minit. — Say that the bus is thirty minutes late.

### B1 · Unit 6: Health, Pharmacy and Emergencies

Describe symptoms, ask for medical help, understand simple instructions, and respond in an emergency.

**Grammar focus:** Body and symptom constructions with sakit; duration with sejak and selama; necessity with perlu; advice with sebaiknya.

**Can-do outcomes:** Describe common symptoms; Ask for a clinic or pharmacy; Communicate essential emergency information.

**Speaking performance:** Explain symptoms at a clinic and understand a basic medicine schedule.

**Vocabulary:** `sakit` — sick / painful, `demam` — fever, `batuk` — cough, `pening` — dizzy, `ubat` — medicine, `doktor` — doctor, `klinik` — clinic, `kecemasan` — emergency, `alahan` — allergy, `rehat` — rest.

**Core sentences:**

- Saya sakit kepala. — Say that you have a headache.
- Saya demam sejak semalam. — Say that you have had a fever since yesterday.
- Saya alah kepada penisilin. — Say that you are allergic to penicillin.
- Di mana klinik yang terdekat? — Ask where the nearest clinic is.
- Saya perlu berjumpa doktor. — Say that you need to see a doctor.
- Ambil ubat ini selepas makan. — Repeat an instruction to take medicine after eating.
- Tolong panggil ambulans. — Ask someone to call an ambulance.
- Saya rasa lebih baik hari ini. — Say that you feel better today.

### B1 · Unit 7: School, Work and Digital Communication

Ask for clarification, discuss deadlines, participate in meetings, and solve common technology problems.

**Grammar focus:** Requests with boleh; obligation with mesti/perlu; future and promises with akan; purpose with untuk; relative yang.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask for academic or workplace help; Discuss tasks and deadlines; Handle simple email, file, and internet issues.

**Speaking performance:** Ask a teacher or colleague for clarification and agree on a deadline.

**Vocabulary:** `kelas` — class, `guru` — teacher, `tugasan` — assignment / task, `mesyuarat` — meeting, `pejabat` — office, `komputer` — computer, `e-mel` — email, `fail` — file, `tarikh akhir` — deadline, `bantuan` — help / assistance.

**Core sentences:**

- Saya tidak faham soalan ini. — Say that you do not understand the question.
- Boleh terangkan sekali lagi? — Ask for another explanation.
- Bila tarikh akhir tugasan ini? — Ask when the deadline is.
- Saya akan hantar e-mel petang ini. — Say that you will send an email this afternoon.
- Mesyuarat bermula pada pukul sepuluh. — Say that the meeting starts at ten.
- Boleh kongsi fail itu dengan saya? — Ask someone to share the file.
- Sambungan internet tidak berfungsi. — Report that the internet is not working.
- Mari kita bekerjasama. — Invite the group to work together.

### B2 · Unit 8: Social Life, Culture and Community

Give and respond to invitations, discuss customs respectfully, and participate in neighbourhood or community activities.

**Grammar focus:** Polite acceptance and refusal; sebab/kerana; conditionals with kalau; reciprocal and inclusive forms with kita.

**Can-do outcomes:** Accept or decline invitations naturally; Ask respectful questions about customs; Discuss community participation.

**Speaking performance:** Invite a neighbour to a celebration, explain a custom, and respond to a dietary question.

**Vocabulary:** `jemputan` — invitation, `majlis` — event / ceremony, `jiran` — neighbour, `komuniti` — community, `perayaan` — celebration / festival, `hadiah` — gift, `tradisi` — tradition, `sopan` — polite, `berkongsi` — share, `sukarelawan` — volunteer.

**Core sentences:**

- Terima kasih atas jemputan. Saya akan datang. — Accept an invitation warmly.
- Maaf, saya sudah ada rancangan pada hari itu. — Decline politely because you already have plans.
- Apa yang patut saya bawa? — Ask what you should bring.
- Saya membawa sedikit hadiah untuk tuan rumah. — Say that you brought a small gift.
- Ada adat yang perlu saya tahu? — Ask whether there is a custom you should know.
- Biasanya tetamu menanggalkan kasut sebelum masuk. — Explain that guests normally remove their shoes.
- Mari sertai aktiviti komuniti bersama-sama. — Invite someone to join a community activity.
- Saya ingin menjadi sukarelawan. — Say that you would like to volunteer.

### B2 · Unit 9: Opinions, Plans and Problem-Solving

Express and qualify opinions, compare options, suggest solutions, and reach a shared decision.

**Grammar focus:** Opinion frames; agreement and soft disagreement; conditionals; sebab-akibat; modal language mungkin, boleh, patut, perlu.

**Can-do outcomes:** Give reasons for an opinion; Disagree without sounding rude; Propose and evaluate solutions.

**Speaking performance:** Discuss a group problem, compare two solutions, and agree on an action plan.

**Vocabulary:** `pendapat` — opinion, `setuju` — agree, `tidak setuju` — disagree, `cadangan` — suggestion, `rancangan` — plan, `masalah` — problem, `penyelesaian` — solution, `mungkin` — maybe / possibly, `sebab` — reason, `keputusan` — decision.

**Core sentences:**

- Pada pendapat saya, pilihan ini lebih praktikal. — Introduce your opinion.
- Saya setuju kerana kosnya lebih rendah. — Agree and give a reason.
- Saya kurang bersetuju dengan cadangan itu. — Disagree gently.
- Apa kata kita cuba cara yang lain? — Suggest a different approach.
- Jika masa tidak cukup, kita boleh kurangkan skop. — Offer a conditional solution.
- Kita perlu mempertimbangkan kesannya kepada pelajar. — Say that one factor needs consideration.
- Mari kita buat keputusan bersama-sama. — Invite the group to decide.
- Rancangan itu berubah kerana cuaca buruk. — Explain that the plan has changed.

### C1 · Unit 10: Meetings, Presentations and Negotiation

Lead structured discussions, present evidence, clarify positions, negotiate constraints, and confirm next actions.

**Grammar focus:** Formal discourse markers; passive constructions; nominalisation; diplomatic requests and concessions.

**Can-do outcomes:** Open and structure a meeting; Present and interpret evidence; Negotiate while preserving cooperation.

**Speaking performance:** Lead a five-minute project meeting and negotiate a revised deadline with clear action items.

**Vocabulary:** `agenda` — agenda, `objektif` — objective, `pembentangan` — presentation, `data` — data, `kemajuan` — progress, `cabaran` — challenge, `bajet` — budget, `rundingan` — negotiation, `kompromi` — compromise, `tindakan` — action.

**Core sentences:**

- Terima kasih kerana hadir. Objektif mesyuarat hari ini ialah menyemak kemajuan projek. — Open a meeting and state the objective.
- Mari kita mulakan dengan perkara pertama dalam agenda. — Move to the first agenda item.
- Berdasarkan data terkini, kadar penyertaan meningkat sebanyak dua puluh peratus. — Present a key data finding.
- Boleh jelaskan bagaimana angka itu dikira? — Ask a speaker to clarify a point.
- Walaupun terdapat beberapa cabaran, projek masih berada di landasan yang betul. — Acknowledge a challenge without stopping the plan.
- Kami mencadangkan lanjutan satu minggu agar kualiti hasil dapat dikekalkan. — Negotiate additional time.
- Sebagai kompromi, kami boleh menyerahkan bahagian pertama pada hari Jumaat. — Offer a compromise.
- Sebelum kita tamat, mari kita sahkan tindakan dan pihak yang bertanggungjawab. — Close with action items.

### C1 · Unit 11: News, Society and the Environment

Discuss public issues, distinguish claims from evidence, explain consequences, and propose balanced responses.

**Grammar focus:** Cause and effect; hedging; evidence frames; passive voice; complex connectors such as sehubungan dengan itu and walau bagaimanapun.

**Can-do outcomes:** Summarise a public issue; Evaluate evidence and source reliability; Explain social or environmental trade-offs.

**Speaking performance:** Summarise a news report, evaluate its evidence, and discuss one policy response.

**Vocabulary:** `berita` — news, `masyarakat` — society, `ekonomi` — economy, `pendidikan` — education, `kesihatan awam` — public health, `alam sekitar` — environment, `pencemaran` — pollution, `perubahan iklim` — climate change, `sumber` — source / resource, `dasar` — policy.

**Core sentences:**

- Laporan ini menumpukan kesan kenaikan kos terhadap keluarga berpendapatan rendah. — Introduce the main point of a report.
- Kita perlu menilai sama ada sumber tersebut boleh dipercayai. — Question the reliability of a source.
- Dakwaan itu disokong oleh data daripada beberapa kajian bebas. — Refer to supporting evidence.
- Pencemaran udara boleh menjejaskan kesihatan awam dalam jangka panjang. — Explain a consequence.
- Walaupun trend ini membimbangkan, datanya masih terhad. — Qualify a claim.
- Dari sudut ekonomi, langkah itu mungkin meningkatkan kos dalam jangka pendek. — Present another perspective.
- Kerajaan boleh memperluas pengangkutan awam dan menggalakkan penggunaan tenaga bersih. — Propose a policy response.
- Sehubungan dengan itu, tindakan pencegahan perlu dilaksanakan dengan segera. — Link the evidence to a conclusion.

### C1+ · Unit 12: Nuance, Idioms, Debate and Storytelling

Sound more natural, recognise common idioms, organise persuasive arguments, and tell coherent personal or professional stories.

**Grammar focus:** Register shifts; idiomatic meaning; concession; emphasis; narrative sequencing; stance and rebuttal.

**Can-do outcomes:** Use common idioms appropriately; Clarify nuanced meaning; Build and rebut an argument; Tell a well-sequenced story.

**Speaking performance:** Tell a three-minute story, then defend a position in a respectful mini-debate.

**Vocabulary:** `ambil berat` — care about / show concern, `ringan tulang` — hardworking and helpful, `buah tangan` — souvenir / gift from a trip, `makan angin` — take a leisure trip, `besar hati` — pleased / honoured, `kecil hati` — hurt or offended, `naik angin` — become angry, `bagai aur dengan tebing` — support one another, `pendirian` — stance / position, `kesimpulan` — conclusion.

**Core sentences:**

- Apa yang saya maksudkan ialah kita perlu lebih berhati-hati. — Clarify your real meaning.
- Sejujurnya, saya kurang yakin bahawa rancangan itu realistik. — Make a candid but respectful comment.
- Walaupun cadangan itu mempunyai kelemahan, manfaatnya masih ketara. — Introduce a concession.
- Dari sudut yang lain, perubahan ini boleh membuka peluang baharu. — Present another angle.
- Izinkan saya memberikan satu contoh yang berkaitan. — Introduce an example.
- Saya faham hujah itu, tetapi bukti yang ada menunjukkan sebaliknya. — Acknowledge and rebut an argument.
- Kesimpulannya, penyelesaian terbaik ialah pendekatan yang berperingkat. — State a conclusion.
- Pada mulanya saya ragu-ragu, kemudian saya mencuba, dan akhirnya saya berjaya. — Sequence a story.

## Assessment plan

### Checkpoints after Units 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10

Learners complete a two-minute role-play and answer five follow-up questions. Score pronunciation/clarity, task completion, vocabulary range, sentence control, and interaction from 1–4 each.

### Final speaking assessment after Unit 12

1. **Daily-life task (3 minutes):** handle one unseen scenario such as a hotel problem, clinic visit, workplace deadline, or invitation.
2. **Prepared talk (3 minutes):** explain a personal experience or community issue with a beginning, development, and conclusion.
3. **Discussion (4 minutes):** give an opinion, respond to a counterargument, and agree on a practical next step.

**Suggested pass standard:** 60/100 overall with no category below 50%. **Advanced completion:** 80/100 with sustained interaction and appropriate formal/informal register.

## Teacher routine for every challenge

1. Learner listens to all options.
2. Learner selects the answer.
3. Learner repeats the correct sentence twice.
4. Teacher changes one detail: person, place, time, quantity, reason, or tense/aspect marker.
5. Learner answers again without reading.

## Platform note

The current Learning Quest schema records multiple-choice/assist answers rather than microphone speech recognition. The course is therefore speaking-first through listen-and-repeat, sentence choice, dialogue completion, and teacher/partner role-play.
