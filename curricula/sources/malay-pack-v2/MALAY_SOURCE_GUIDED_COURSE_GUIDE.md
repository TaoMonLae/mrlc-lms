# Modern Spoken Malay: Source-Guided Course

**Course code:** `MRLC-MALAY-GOVINFO-GUIDE-V1`  
**Scope:** 12 units, 48 lessons, 384 challenges  
**Levels:** A1-B2  
**Primary historical reference:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-W-96478719d96146672c586f0d55523121/pdf/GOVPUB-W-96478719d96146672c586f0d55523121.pdf

## Purpose

This companion course follows the practical teaching sequence of the 1943 guide while using current Malaysian Malay, current spelling, civilian situations, and respectful modern communication. It is not a transcription of the historical text.

## Lesson cycle

1. **Vocabulary and Audio** - ten current words or expressions.
2. **Daily Speaking** - eight functional sentences.
3. **Scenario Practice** - six dialogue completions.
4. **Sentence and Source Workshop** - eight sentence-building or historical-to-modern activities.

## Unit guide

### A1 - Unit 1: Clear Sounds and Learning Phrases

Build clear pronunciation habits and learn the classroom phrases needed to listen, repeat, slow down, and ask for help.

**Source anchor:** How to Use the Records and Guide; Hints on Pronunciation; Special Points (printed pages 6-10).

**Grammar focus:** polite requests with tolong and boleh; imperatives; lebih + adjective; sekali lagi.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask a speaker to repeat or slow down; Notice common Malay sound patterns; Practise short phrases with steady rhythm.

**Speaking performance:** Complete a two-minute listen-and-repeat coaching exchange with a partner.

**Vocabulary:** `dengar` - listen, `ulang` - repeat, `sebut` - pronounce / say, `jelas` - clear, `perlahan` - slowly, `cepat` - quickly, `bunyi` - sound, `suku kata` - syllable, `intonasi` - intonation, `latihan` - practice.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Boleh ulang sekali lagi?** - Ask someone to repeat.
- **Tolong cakap lebih perlahan.** - Ask someone to speak more slowly.
- **Saya tidak dengar dengan jelas.** - Say that you did not hear clearly.
- **Bagaimana sebutan perkataan ini?** - Ask how a word is pronounced.
- **Apakah maksud perkataan ini?** - Ask what a word means.
- **Sekarang saya faham.** - Say that you understand now.
- **Saya perlu lebih banyak latihan.** - Say that you need more practice.
- **Boleh saya dengar sekali lagi?** - Ask to listen one more time.

### A1 - Unit 2: Greetings, Introductions and Courtesy

Greet people, introduce yourself, exchange names, thank others, apologise, and say goodbye naturally.

**Source anchor:** Greetings and General Phrases; Other Useful Phrases; Additional Expressions (printed pages 11 and 26-30).

**Grammar focus:** personal pronouns; simple identity sentences; siapa and dari mana; bukan for noun negation.

**Can-do outcomes:** Use greetings for different times; Introduce yourself and ask names; Use polite social expressions.

**Speaking performance:** Meet a new neighbour and hold a ninety-second introductory conversation.

**Vocabulary:** `selamat pagi` - good morning, `selamat petang` - good evening, `apa khabar` - how are you, `terima kasih` - thank you, `maaf` - sorry / excuse me, `tolong` - please / help, `nama` - name, `kawan` - friend, `selamat jalan` - goodbye to a person leaving, `selamat tinggal` - goodbye said by the person leaving.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Selamat pagi.** - Greet someone in the morning.
- **Apa khabar?** - Ask how someone is.
- **Khabar baik, terima kasih.** - Say that you are well.
- **Siapa nama awak?** - Ask for the person’s name.
- **Nama saya Nur.** - Introduce yourself as Nur.
- **Awak berasal dari mana?** - Ask where the person is from.
- **Gembira berkenalan dengan awak.** - Say that you are pleased to meet them.
- **Selamat jalan.** - Say goodbye to someone who is leaving.

### A1 - Unit 3: Numbers, Time and Days

Count, ask prices and quantities, tell the time, name the days, and arrange simple appointments.

**Source anchor:** Numbers; Money; Time (printed pages 15-17 and 22-25).

**Grammar focus:** number order; pukul for clock time; pada for scheduled time; lebih, suku, setengah, kurang.

**Can-do outcomes:** Use numbers in everyday exchanges; Tell and ask the time; State days and simple schedules.

**Speaking performance:** Arrange a meeting time and confirm the day, hour, and number of people.

**Vocabulary:** `satu` - one, `dua` - two, `tiga` - three, `sepuluh` - ten, `seratus` - one hundred, `pukul` - clock-time marker, `setengah` - half, `suku` - quarter, `hari` - day, `sekarang` - now.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Pukul berapa sekarang?** - Ask the current time.
- **Sekarang pukul tiga.** - Say that it is three o’clock.
- **Sekarang pukul setengah tujuh.** - Say that it is half past six.
- **Kelas bermula pada pukul sembilan.** - Say that the class begins at nine.
- **Hari ini hari apa?** - Ask what day it is today.
- **Hari ini hari Rabu.** - Say that today is Wednesday.
- **Kereta api bertolak pukul berapa?** - Ask when the train departs.
- **Mesyuarat itu esok.** - Say that the meeting is tomorrow.

### A1 - Unit 4: Objects and Simple Questions

Identify objects, ask what something is called, describe basic qualities, and form simple positive and negative sentences.

**Source anchor:** What’s This?; Asking for Things; Fill-in Sentences (printed pages 18-20 and 31-45).

**Grammar focus:** apa ini/itu; demonstratives; noun possession; ada and tidak ada; adjective predicates.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask and answer what an object is; Describe common qualities; Ask whether something is available.

**Speaking performance:** Identify objects in a room and ask for three items you need.

**Vocabulary:** `ini` - this, `itu` - that, `apa` - what, `ada` - there is / have, `tiada` - there is not / none, `besar` - big, `kecil` - small, `bersih` - clean, `kotor` - dirty, `nama` - name.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Apa ini?** - Ask what this is.
- **Ini cawan.** - Say that this is a cup.
- **Apakah nama benda ini dalam bahasa Melayu?** - Ask what the object is called in Malay.
- **Ada pen?** - Ask whether there is a pen.
- **Saya tidak ada pensel.** - Say that you do not have a pencil.
- **Bilik ini bersih.** - Say that the room is clean.
- **Beg itu terlalu besar.** - Say that the bag is too big.
- **Yang mana satu kepunyaan awak?** - Ask which one is yours.

### A2 - Unit 5: Food, Drink and Ordering

Ask for food and drink, order a meal, describe taste, request changes, and explain simple dietary needs.

**Source anchor:** Asking for Things; food and drink vocabulary; buying examples (printed pages 19-21 and 31-37).

**Grammar focus:** mahu/hendak; classifiers secawan, segelas, sepinggan; jangan; kurang and lebih.

**Can-do outcomes:** Order common food and drinks; Use quantity expressions; Make polite changes to an order.

**Speaking performance:** Order a meal at a Malaysian food stall, change one item, and ask for the bill.

**Vocabulary:** `nasi` - rice, `ikan` - fish, `ayam` - chicken, `sayur` - vegetables, `buah` - fruit, `air kosong` - plain water, `kopi` - coffee, `teh` - tea, `lapar` - hungry, `dahaga` - thirsty.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Saya mahu nasi dan ikan.** - Order rice and fish.
- **Boleh beri saya segelas air kosong?** - Ask for one glass of water.
- **Saya mahu secawan kopi.** - Order a cup of coffee.
- **Tolong kurangkan gula.** - Ask for less sugar.
- **Tolong jangan pedas.** - Ask for the food not to be spicy.
- **Saya tidak makan daging.** - Say that you do not eat meat.
- **Ada makanan vegetarian?** - Ask whether vegetarian food is available.
- **Boleh saya minta bil?** - Ask for the bill.

### A2 - Unit 6: Shopping, Prices and Money

Ask prices, compare products, request sizes or quantities, negotiate politely, pay, and ask for a receipt.

**Source anchor:** Buying Things; Money; Fill-in Sentences and shopping vocabulary (printed pages 21-22 and 31-36).

**Grammar focus:** berapa; harganya; lebih + adjective; terlalu; payment with tunai/kad; classifiers.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask and understand prices; Compare size and quality; Complete a simple purchase.

**Speaking performance:** Buy clothing and personal supplies at a market using cash or card.

**Vocabulary:** `harga` - price, `ringgit` - Malaysian ringgit, `sen` - sen, `murah` - cheap, `mahal` - expensive, `saiz` - size, `tunai` - cash, `kad` - card, `resit` - receipt, `baki` - change / balance.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Berapa harganya?** - Ask how much an item costs.
- **Barang ini terlalu mahal.** - Say that the item is too expensive.
- **Ada yang lebih murah?** - Ask whether there is a cheaper one.
- **Ada saiz yang lebih besar?** - Ask for a larger size.
- **Saya mahu dua unit.** - Ask for two of the items.
- **Boleh kurang sedikit?** - Politely ask for a small discount.
- **Saya akan bayar dengan kad.** - Say that you will pay by card.
- **Boleh saya dapatkan resit?** - Ask for a receipt.

### A2 - Unit 7: Places, Distance and Directions

Ask where places are, understand distance, follow landmarks, and give clear walking directions.

**Source anchor:** Location; Directions; distance and place frames (printed pages 13-14, 29 and 39-41).

**Grammar focus:** di mana; ke mana; dari sini; dekat/jauh; imperatives belok, jalan, terus.

**Can-do outcomes:** Ask for common places; Follow left/right/straight directions; Describe distance and landmarks.

**Speaking performance:** Ask for directions from a market to a clinic and repeat the route back.

**Vocabulary:** `di mana` - where, `kiri` - left, `kanan` - right, `terus` - straight, `dekat` - near, `jauh` - far, `simpang` - junction, `jambatan` - bridge, `pasar` - market, `jalan` - road / walk.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Di mana pasar?** - Ask where the market is.
- **Berapa jauh bandar dari sini?** - Ask how far the town is.
- **Adakah klinik itu dekat?** - Ask whether the clinic is near.
- **Jalan terus.** - Tell someone to go straight.
- **Belok kiri.** - Tell someone to turn left.
- **Belok kanan di simpang.** - Tell someone to turn right at the junction.
- **Bangunan itu di sebelah bank.** - Say that the building is beside the bank.
- **Boleh tunjukkan tempat itu pada peta?** - Ask someone to show the place on a map.

### A2 - Unit 8: Transport, Accommodation and Public Signs

Use public transport, ask departure times, check into a room, and understand common signs in modern Malaysia.

**Source anchor:** Time and travel expressions; place vocabulary; Important Signs (printed pages 23-25, 39-47).

**Grammar focus:** akan and belum; departure/arrival verbs; location phrases; prohibitions with dilarang.

**Can-do outcomes:** Buy and confirm transport details; Handle simple accommodation needs; Recognise important public signs.

**Speaking performance:** Travel by train, check into a hotel, and report one room problem.

**Vocabulary:** `bas` - bus, `kereta api` - train, `stesen` - station, `tiket` - ticket, `bertolak` - depart, `tiba` - arrive, `hotel` - hotel, `bilik` - room, `masuk` - entrance / enter, `keluar` - exit / leave.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Saya mahu satu tiket ke Kuala Lumpur.** - Buy one ticket to Kuala Lumpur.
- **Kereta api bertolak pukul berapa?** - Ask what time the train departs.
- **Di platform mana?** - Ask which platform to use.
- **Bas belum tiba.** - Say that the bus has not arrived yet.
- **Saya ada tempahan hotel.** - Say that you have a hotel reservation.
- **Boleh saya daftar masuk?** - Ask to check in.
- **Tiada air panas di bilik saya.** - Report that there is no hot water.
- **Di mana jalan keluar?** - Ask where the exit is.

### B1 - Unit 9: Personal Needs and Everyday Services

Ask for personal items, find services, arrange repairs, and explain practical needs at home or while travelling.

**Source anchor:** Fill-in Sentences: personal items, clothing, services and places (printed pages 31-41).

**Grammar focus:** perlukan; di mana saya boleh; ada/tidak ada; passive and service requests with boleh dibaiki.

**Can-do outcomes:** Request essential personal items; Locate useful services; Explain a repair or service problem.

**Speaking performance:** Find a pharmacy, laundry, and repair shop while explaining what you need at each place.

**Vocabulary:** `tuala` - towel, `bantal` - pillow, `selimut` - blanket, `berus gigi` - toothbrush, `ubat gigi` - toothpaste, `farmasi` - pharmacy, `dobi` - laundry, `bengkel` - workshop / garage, `kedai gunting rambut` - barbershop / salon, `pejabat pos` - post office.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Saya perlukan tuala yang bersih.** - Say that you need a clean towel.
- **Ada selimut tambahan?** - Ask whether there is an extra blanket.
- **Di mana saya boleh membeli ubat gigi?** - Ask where you can buy toothpaste.
- **Di mana farmasi yang terdekat?** - Ask where the nearest pharmacy is.
- **Boleh baju ini dicuci hari ini?** - Ask whether the shirt can be washed today.
- **Kasut saya rosak.** - Say that your shoe is damaged.
- **Saya mahu potong rambut.** - Ask for a haircut.
- **Di mana saya boleh membaiki basikal?** - Ask where you can repair a bicycle.

### B1 - Unit 10: Health, Safety and Emergencies

Describe symptoms, ask for medical help, communicate allergies, and respond to common safety situations.

**Source anchor:** Additional Expressions and health/service frames (printed pages 27-30 and 36-45). Military-specific commands were removed.

**Grammar focus:** sakit + body part; sejak/selama; perlu; imperative safety language; jangan and dilarang.

**Can-do outcomes:** Describe basic symptoms; Request urgent help; Understand essential safety instructions.

**Speaking performance:** Explain symptoms at a clinic and respond to a simple emergency outside.

**Vocabulary:** `sakit` - sick / painful, `demam` - fever, `pening` - dizzy, `ubat` - medicine, `doktor` - doctor, `klinik` - clinic, `ambulans` - ambulance, `bahaya` - danger, `awas` - watch out / caution, `bantuan` - help.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Saya sakit kepala.** - Say that you have a headache.
- **Saya demam sejak semalam.** - Say that you have had a fever since yesterday.
- **Saya berasa pening.** - Say that you feel dizzy.
- **Saya alah kepada penisilin.** - Say that you are allergic to penicillin.
- **Di mana klinik yang terdekat?** - Ask where the nearest clinic is.
- **Saya perlu berjumpa doktor.** - Say that you need a doctor.
- **Tolong panggil ambulans.** - Ask someone to call an ambulance.
- **Awas! Hati-hati!** - Warn someone to be careful.

### B1 - Unit 11: Reusable Sentence Frames

Combine high-frequency frames with new nouns, verbs, places, and adjectives to create many useful sentences.

**Source anchor:** Fill-in Sentences (printed pages 31-45), reworked as productive modern-Malay sentence patterns.

**Grammar focus:** mahu/perlu; boleh; ada; di mana; adjective predicates; negation; question formation.

**Can-do outcomes:** Produce new sentences from flexible frames; Ask for missing information; Repair communication when vocabulary is limited.

**Speaking performance:** Solve five unseen daily problems by adapting sentence frames without reading a script.

**Vocabulary:** `mahu` - want, `perlu` - need, `boleh` - can / may, `mendapatkan` - obtain / get, `membawa` - bring / carry, `memberi` - give, `mencari` - look for, `mempunyai` - have / possess, `terlalu` - too / excessively, `barangkali` - perhaps.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Saya mahu satu tiket.** - Use the frame “I want ...” with a ticket.
- **Saya perlukan bantuan.** - Use the frame “I need ...” with help.
- **Di mana saya boleh mendapatkan air minuman?** - Use the frame “Where can I get ...?” with drinking water.
- **Awak ada peta?** - Use the frame “Do you have ...?” with a map.
- **Tolong bawakan saya sebuah kerusi.** - Use the frame “Please bring me ...” with a chair.
- **Ini terlalu panas.** - Use the frame “This is too ...” with hot.
- **Saya tidak mempunyai wang tunai.** - Use the frame “I do not have ...” with cash.
- **Ada bank berdekatan?** - Use the frame “Is there ... nearby?” with a bank.

### B2 - Unit 12: Extended Scenarios and Malay Then and Now

Manage longer real-life conversations and recognise how the source guide’s 1943 spelling, register, and context differ from current Malaysian Malay.

**Source anchor:** Important Signs and Alphabetical Word List (printed pages 46-79), plus examples throughout the guide.

**Grammar focus:** register choice; discourse sequencing; explanation and clarification; old-to-modern spelling recognition.

**Can-do outcomes:** Sustain multi-step service conversations; Explain and resolve misunderstandings; Recognise common historical spellings without using them as modern standards.

**Speaking performance:** Complete an integrated travel day and explain three old-to-modern language changes.

**Vocabulary:** `dahulu` - formerly / in the past, `sekarang` - now, `ejaan` - spelling, `baku` - standard, `ragam bahasa` - language register, `rasmi` - formal / official, `santai` - casual, `konteks` - context, `menjelaskan` - explain, `menyesuaikan` - adapt.

**Core speaking sentences:**

- **Sumber itu menggunakan sistem ejaan lama.** - Explain that the source uses old spelling.
- **Kursus ini menggunakan bahasa Melayu Malaysia semasa.** - Say that the course uses current Malaysian Malay.
- **Dalam sumber lama, “saja” digunakan untuk ejaan moden “saya”.** - Explain that “saja” in the source is now written “saya.”
- **Ejaan lama “djalan” kini ditulis “jalan”.** - Explain that “djalan” is now “jalan.”
- **Maaf, maksud saya bukan begitu.** - Politely clarify a misunderstanding.
- **Boleh jelaskan konteksnya?** - Ask someone to explain the context.
- **Bentuk santai itu tidak sesuai untuk surat rasmi.** - Say that a casual form is unsuitable for a formal letter.
- **Panduan lama itu berguna sebagai rekod sejarah dan sumber tema komunikasi harian.** - Summarise the practical value of the old guide.

## Teaching notes

- Use the historical forms only for recognition in the final unit. Do not mark them as preferred current spelling.
- Ask learners to repeat each correct `audioText`, then change one detail such as time, place, quantity, or person.
- Pair every digital lesson with a short unscripted role-play.
- For assessment, score clarity, task completion, vocabulary, sentence control, interaction, and appropriate register.

## Suggested assessment

- **A1 checkpoint:** introductions, time, objects, and simple requests.
- **A2 checkpoint:** restaurant, shopping, directions, transport, accommodation, and signs.
- **B1 checkpoint:** services, clinic communication, emergencies, and flexible sentence frames.
- **B2 final:** one integrated travel/service scenario plus an explanation of old-to-modern Malay spelling and context.
