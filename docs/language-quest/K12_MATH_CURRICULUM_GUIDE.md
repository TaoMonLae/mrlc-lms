# K-12 Mathematics Curriculum Guide

## Design principles

The course family treats mathematics as a language of quantities, patterns, relationships, space, data, and change. Each unit moves through three repeated lesson phases:

1. **Understand the Idea** - meaning, representations, vocabulary, and connections.
2. **Build the Skill** - accurate procedures, fluency, and strategic practice.
3. **Apply and Explain** - word problems, reasoning, checking, and communication.

The design is informed by the Common Core Mathematics Standards emphasis on focused progressions, conceptual understanding, procedural skill, mathematical practices, and real-world problem solving. It remains adaptable to other K-12 systems.

## Language Quest interaction design

- **SELECT / ASSIST / CLOZE:** calculations, concepts, and missing values.
- **GRAMMAR_TRANSFORM:** equivalent forms and algebraic transformations.
- **MATCHING:** equivalent representations, vocabulary, formulas, and results.
- **REORDER:** number ordering and multi-step problem-solving processes.
- Every checked answer includes an explanation for feedback and mastery review.
- Audio is intentionally disabled because the core learning object is symbolic and visual.
- Emoji are intentionally absent.

## Scope and sequence

### Kindergarten Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `K.CC; K.OA; K.NBT; K.MD; K.G`

1. **Counting to 10** - Count, name, represent, compare, and order numbers from 0 to 10.
2. **Counting to 20** - Extend counting and number recognition through 20.
3. **Compare and Order** - Use greater than, less than, equal, before, after, and between.
4. **Make and Break Numbers** - Compose and decompose numbers within 10.
5. **Addition Stories** - Model joining and adding within 10.
6. **Subtraction Stories** - Model taking away and comparing within 10.
7. **Shapes and Position** - Recognize common two-dimensional shapes and spatial words.
8. **Measure, Sort, and Describe** - Compare length, weight, capacity, and classify objects.

### Grade 1 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `1.OA; 1.NBT; 1.MD; 1.G`

1. **Number Patterns to 120** - Count, read, and extend number sequences to 120.
2. **Tens and Ones** - Understand two-digit numbers as tens and ones.
3. **Addition within 20** - Use strategies to add fluently within 20.
4. **Subtraction within 20** - Use relationships and strategies to subtract within 20.
5. **Add and Subtract within 100** - Work with two-digit addition and subtraction situations.
6. **Time and Money** - Tell time, recognize Malaysian money, and solve simple money problems.
7. **Measurement and Data** - Compare lengths and interpret simple tally and picture data.
8. **Shapes and Equal Parts** - Reason about shapes, halves, and quarters.

### Grade 2 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `2.OA; 2.NBT; 2.MD; 2.G`

1. **Place Value to 1,000** - Read, compare, and represent three-digit numbers.
2. **Addition and Subtraction within 100** - Build fluency with two-digit operations.
3. **Addition and Subtraction within 1,000** - Use place-value strategies for three-digit operations.
4. **Equal Groups and Arrays** - Build foundations for multiplication and division.
5. **Time, Money, and Measurement** - Solve measurement, elapsed-time, and money situations.
6. **Shapes and Area Foundations** - Use rows, columns, area units, and shape properties.
7. **Fractions as Equal Parts** - Identify and compare halves, thirds, and fourths.
8. **Data and Word Problems** - Represent, interpret, and solve problems with data.

### Grade 3 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `3.OA; 3.NBT; 3.NF; 3.MD; 3.G`

1. **Multiplication Facts** - Understand and develop multiplication facts through 10 × 10.
2. **Division Facts** - Interpret division as sharing, grouping, and inverse multiplication.
3. **Place Value and Rounding** - Use place value and round whole numbers.
4. **Multi-digit Addition and Subtraction** - Add and subtract within 1,000 using standard and mental strategies.
5. **Fractions on a Number Line** - Understand unit fractions, equivalence, and comparison.
6. **Area and Perimeter** - Measure and calculate rectangle area and perimeter.
7. **Measurement and Time** - Use metric units, time intervals, and conversions.
8. **Data and Chance** - Interpret scaled graphs and explore simple chance.

### Grade 4 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `4.OA; 4.NBT; 4.NF; 4.MD; 4.G`

1. **Large Numbers and Rounding** - Read, compare, and round multi-digit whole numbers.
2. **Multi-digit Multiplication** - Multiply multi-digit numbers by one- and two-digit factors.
3. **Division with Remainders** - Use division relationships and interpret remainders.
4. **Fraction Equivalence and Comparison** - Generate equivalent fractions and compare fraction sizes.
5. **Add and Subtract Fractions** - Combine fractions with like denominators and mixed contexts.
6. **Decimal Fractions** - Connect tenths and hundredths to decimal notation.
7. **Angles and Shape Properties** - Measure angles and classify shapes by properties.
8. **Measurement and Data** - Convert units and solve measurement and data problems.

### Grade 5 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `5.OA; 5.NBT; 5.NF; 5.MD; 5.G`

1. **Decimal Place Value** - Read, compare, round, and operate with decimals.
2. **Whole-number Operations** - Use efficient multi-digit multiplication and division.
3. **Add and Subtract Fractions** - Use common denominators and solve fraction problems.
4. **Multiply and Divide Fractions** - Interpret and calculate products and quotients of fractions.
5. **Volume and Measurement** - Find volume and connect formulas to unit cubes.
6. **Coordinate Plane** - Plot points and reason about distances in the first quadrant.
7. **Expressions and Patterns** - Evaluate expressions and analyze numerical patterns.
8. **Data and Problem Solving** - Use measures and plots to solve multi-step problems.

### Grade 6 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `6.RP; 6.NS; 6.EE; 6.G; 6.SP`

1. **Ratios and Unit Rates** - Understand ratio language and calculate unit rates.
2. **Fractions and Decimals** - Compute fluently with positive rational numbers.
3. **Percents** - Connect fractions, decimals, and percent in real situations.
4. **Integers and the Number Line** - Compare and operate with positive and negative numbers.
5. **Expressions** - Write, evaluate, and simplify numerical and algebraic expressions.
6. **Equations and Inequalities** - Solve one-variable equations and inequalities.
7. **Geometry** - Find area, surface area, and volume.
8. **Statistics** - Describe distributions with center and spread.

### Grade 7 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `7.RP; 7.NS; 7.EE; 7.G; 7.SP`

1. **Proportional Relationships** - Recognize and solve proportional relationships.
2. **Rational Number Operations** - Add, subtract, multiply, and divide rational numbers.
3. **Expressions and Equations** - Use equivalent expressions and solve multi-step equations.
4. **Percent Applications** - Solve mark-up, discount, tax, tip, and percent-change problems.
5. **Scale Geometry and Circles** - Use scale, circumference, and area formulas.
6. **Probability** - Model simple and compound chance events.
7. **Statistics and Sampling** - Use random samples and compare populations.
8. **Integrated Modeling** - Apply proportional, statistical, and geometric reasoning.

### Grade 8 Mathematics

Broad reference domains: `8.NS; 8.EE; 8.F; 8.G; 8.SP`

1. **Real Numbers and Exponents** - Work with integer exponents, roots, and scientific notation.
2. **Linear Equations** - Solve linear equations with rational coefficients.
3. **Functions** - Understand functions as input-output relationships.
4. **Systems of Equations** - Solve and interpret pairs of linear equations.
5. **Transformations** - Use translations, rotations, reflections, and dilations.
6. **Pythagorean Theorem** - Apply the Pythagorean theorem in two and three dimensions.
7. **Geometry and Volume** - Use volume formulas for cylinders, cones, and spheres.
8. **Bivariate Data** - Analyze associations, scatter plots, and lines of fit.

### Grade 9 Mathematics: Algebra I

Broad reference domains: `N-Q; A-SSE; A-CED; A-REI; F-IF; S-ID`

1. **Algebra Foundations** - Evaluate expressions and reason with algebraic structure.
2. **Linear Equations and Inequalities** - Solve, graph, and interpret linear constraints.
3. **Linear Functions** - Represent linear functions with tables, equations, and graphs.
4. **Systems and Modeling** - Solve systems and interpret intersections.
5. **Exponents and Radicals** - Use exponent laws and simplify radical expressions.
6. **Polynomials** - Add, subtract, multiply, and factor polynomials.
7. **Quadratic Functions** - Solve and interpret quadratic equations and graphs.
8. **Statistics and Data Modeling** - Use scatter plots, lines of fit, and data summaries.

### Grade 10 Mathematics: Geometry

Broad reference domains: `G-CO; G-SRT; G-GPE; G-C; G-GMD; S-CP`

1. **Foundations, Logic, and Angles** - Use definitions, angle relationships, and deductive reasoning.
2. **Transformations and Congruence** - Establish congruence through rigid transformations.
3. **Similarity and Dilations** - Use similarity criteria, scale factors, and proportions.
4. **Triangle Relationships** - Apply right-triangle and general triangle relationships.
5. **Coordinate Geometry** - Use slope, distance, midpoint, and equations of lines.
6. **Circles** - Reason with radius, chords, arcs, tangents, and circle equations.
7. **Area, Surface Area, and Volume** - Apply two- and three-dimensional measurement formulas.
8. **Probability in Geometry** - Use geometric probability and conditional reasoning.

### Grade 11 Mathematics: Algebra II

Broad reference domains: `A-APR; A-REI; F-IF; F-BF; F-LE; S-ID; S-CP`

1. **Function Transformations** - Transform, combine, compose, and invert functions.
2. **Polynomial and Rational Functions** - Analyze zeros, factors, and rational behavior.
3. **Quadratics and Complex Structure** - Solve quadratic equations and interpret structure.
4. **Exponential and Logarithmic Functions** - Model growth and solve exponential equations.
5. **Sequences and Series** - Use arithmetic and geometric sequences and sums.
6. **Trigonometric Functions** - Use right-triangle and unit-circle definitions.
7. **Probability and Statistics** - Use distributions, combinations, and conditional probability.
8. **Mathematical Modeling** - Build and critique models from real data.

### Grade 12 Mathematics: Precalculus and Calculus Readiness

Broad reference domains: `F-TF; N-VM; A-APR; S-MD; Precalculus/Calculus extension`

1. **Advanced Functions** - Analyze compositions, inverses, transformations, and domains.
2. **Trigonometric Identities** - Use identities, equations, and periodic models.
3. **Vectors and Conic Sections** - Work with vectors and standard conic equations.
4. **Limits and Continuity** - Develop intuitive and algebraic limit reasoning.
5. **Introduction to Derivatives** - Interpret and calculate rates of change.
6. **Introduction to Integrals** - Interpret accumulation and find basic antiderivatives.
7. **Financial Mathematics** - Use percentages, interest, loans, savings, and budgets.
8. **Capstone Modeling** - Select functions, justify assumptions, and communicate solutions.

## Suggested implementation

- Assign the grade-level course as the classroom focus course.
- Use one unit every three to five weeks depending on timetable and learner support needs.
- Require at least 80% lesson accuracy before moving to the next unit.
- Use mastery review for missed skills and boss battles as cumulative unit checks.
- Pair Language Quest work with teacher-led manipulatives, diagrams, written working, projects, and discussion.

## Assessment model

A complete grade contains 144 activities. Suggested interpretation:

- 90-100%: secure mastery
- 80-89%: ready to progress with light review
- 65-79%: targeted practice needed
- Below 65%: reteach concepts with concrete and visual support
